World general knowledge from oldest to latest spans the entire timeline of the universe, Earth, life, and human civilization. The oldest known event in the universe is the Big Bang, which occurred approximately 13.8 billion years ago, marking the beginning of space, time, matter, and energy. The oldest known stars formed about 13.6 billion years ago. The oldest known galaxy, GN-z11, formed about 13.4 billion years ago. Earth formed about 4.54 billion years ago from the accretion of dust and gas around the young Sun. The oldest known rocks on Earth are the Acasta Gneisses in Canada, dated to about 4.03 billion years ago. The oldest known minerals are zircons from Western Australia, dated to 4.4 billion years ago. The Moon formed about 4.5 billion years ago, likely from a giant impact between Earth and a Mars sized body called Theia. The oldest known evidence of life on Earth comes from stromatolites in Western Australia, dated to about 3.5 billion years ago, created by cyanobacteria. The oldest known fossils of multicellular life are from the Francevillian biota in Gabon, dated to about 2.1 billion years ago. The oldest known complex multicellular organisms, the Ediacaran biota, appeared about 600 million years ago. The Cambrian explosion, about 541 million years ago, saw the rapid diversification of most major animal phyla. The oldest known fish appeared about 530 million years ago. The oldest known land plants appeared about 470 million years ago. The oldest known amphibians appeared about 370 million years ago. The oldest known reptiles appeared about 315 million years ago. The oldest known dinosaurs appeared about 230 million years ago during the Triassic period. The oldest known mammals appeared about 225 million years ago, small shrew like creatures. The oldest known birds appeared about 150 million years ago, with Archaeopteryx being a famous example. The oldest known flowering plants, angiosperms, appeared about 140 million years ago. The extinction of non avian dinosaurs occurred about 66 million years ago, caused by a massive asteroid impact. The oldest known primates appeared about 55 million years ago. The oldest known hominins, the evolutionary branch leading to humans, appeared about 6 to 7 million years ago with Sahelanthropus tchadensis. The oldest known stone tools, found in Kenya, date to about 3.3 million years ago, made by Australopithecus or Kenyanthropus. The oldest known control of fire by hominins dates to about 1.5 million years ago. The oldest known Homo sapiens fossils are from Omo Kibish in Ethiopia, dated to about 233,000 years ago. The oldest known cave art, such as the hand stencils in Sulawesi, Indonesia, dates to about 45,500 years ago. The oldest known musical instruments, bone flutes from Germany, date to about 42,000 years ago, made by early modern humans. The oldest known pottery, from Xianrendong Cave in China, dates to about 20,000 years ago. The oldest known domesticated plant, fig trees, were cultivated in the Jordan Valley about 11,400 years ago. The oldest known domesticated animal, the dog, was domesticated from wolves about 15,000 years ago. The Neolithic Revolution, the transition from hunting and gathering to agriculture and settled life, began about 12,000 years ago in the Fertile Crescent of the Middle East. The oldest known permanent settlements include Jericho in the West Bank, inhabited since about 11,000 BCE, and Çatalhöyük in Turkey, inhabited from about 7500 BCE. The oldest known city is often considered to be Uruk in Mesopotamia, founded about 4500 BCE, which had tens of thousands of inhabitants. The oldest known civilization is Sumer, which emerged in Mesopotamia between the Tigris and Euphrates rivers around 4500 BCE. The Sumerians invented the first writing system, cuneiform, around 3400 BCE. The oldest known written text is the Kish Tablet from Sumer, dating to about 3500 BCE. The oldest known named person in recorded history is Kushim, a Sumerian accountant who signed a tablet around 3400 BCE. The oldest known law code is the Code of Ur Nammu, written in Sumerian around 2100 BCE, predating Hammurabi's code by about 300 years. Hammurabi's Code, from Babylon around 1754 BCE, is the most famous ancient law code with its eye for an eye principle. The oldest known peace treaty is the Treaty of Kadesh, between the Egyptian pharaoh Ramesses II and the Hittite king Hattusili III, signed around 1259 BCE. The oldest known alphabet is the Proto Sinaitic script, developed around 1850 BCE in the Sinai Peninsula, from which nearly all modern alphabets descend. The oldest known continuously inhabited city is Damascus, Syria, which has been inhabited since at least 6300 BCE. The oldest known surviving building still in use is the Pantheon in Rome, completed around 126 CE, still used as a church. The oldest known surviving wooden building is the Horyuji Temple in Japan, built around 607 CE. The oldest known surviving bridge still in use is the Arkadiko Bridge in Greece, built around 1300 BCE. The oldest known religion is Hinduism, which has roots dating back to the Indus Valley Civilization around 3000 BCE, though its scriptures, the Vedas, were composed from about 1500 BCE. The oldest known monotheistic religion is Zoroastrianism, founded by the prophet Zoroaster in ancient Persia, dated to roughly 1200 to 1500 BCE. The oldest known sacred texts are the Pyramid Texts of ancient Egypt, inscribed on pyramid walls around 2400 BCE. The oldest known surviving complete book is the Diamond Sutra, a Buddhist text printed in China in 868 CE. The oldest known library is the Library of Ashurbanipal in Nineveh, Assyria, dating to the 7th century BCE, containing over 30,000 cuneiform tablets. The oldest known university is the University of Al Quaraouiyine in Fez, Morocco, founded in 859 CE, still operating. The oldest known medical text is the Edwin Smith Papyrus from Egypt, dating to about 1600 BCE, describing surgical procedures. The oldest known map is the Babylonian World Map, a clay tablet from about 600 BCE. The oldest known calendar is the lunar calendar used by the Sumerians around 3000 BCE. The Egyptian solar calendar, with 365 days, was developed around 2800 BCE. The oldest known wheel was invented in Mesopotamia around 3500 BCE, first used for pottery, then for carts. The oldest known plow was invented in Mesopotamia around 3000 BCE. The oldest known sailboat was developed in Egypt around 3200 BCE. The oldest known metalworking began with copper, around 5000 BCE in the Balkans and Middle East. The Bronze Age began around 3300 BCE with the alloying of copper and tin. The Iron Age began around 1200 BCE in Anatolia. The oldest known currency was the shekel, a weight of barley used in Mesopotamia around 3000 BCE. The first coins were minted in the Kingdom of Lydia, in modern Turkey, around 600 BCE. The oldest known mathematical text is the Rhind Mathematical Papyrus from Egypt, dated to 1650 BCE. The oldest known geometry is found in the Indus Valley Civilization around 2500 BCE. The oldest known astronomical records are Babylonian clay tablets from about 1800 BCE. The oldest known clock is the sundial, used in ancient Egypt and Babylon around 1500 BCE. The water clock, or clepsydra, was invented in Egypt around 1400 BCE. The oldest known mechanical computer is the Antikythera mechanism, a Greek device from about 100 BCE that calculated astronomical positions. The oldest known compass was invented in China during the Han Dynasty, around 200 BCE, using lodestone. The oldest known paper was invented in China during the Han Dynasty, around 105 CE, by Cai Lun. The oldest known printing was woodblock printing in China around 200 CE. The oldest known movable type printing was invented by Bi Sheng in China around 1040 CE, but metal movable type in Korea around 1234 CE. The Gutenberg press, with movable type in Europe, was invented around 1440 CE, revolutionizing information. The oldest known gunpowder formula dates to 9th century China, used in fireworks then weapons. The oldest known telescope was invented by Hans Lippershey in the Netherlands in 1608, and Galileo improved it in 1609. The oldest known microscope was invented by Zacharias Janssen in the Netherlands around 1590. The oldest known thermometer was invented by Galileo Galilei in 1593. The oldest known barometer was invented by Evangelista Torricelli in 1643. The oldest known steam engine was the aeolipile invented by Hero of Alexandria in the 1st century CE, but practical steam engines were developed by Thomas Newcomen in 1712 and James Watt in 1769. The oldest known battery, the Baghdad Battery, dates to about 150 BCE to 650 CE, possibly used for electroplating. The oldest known electric motor was invented by Michael Faraday in 1821. The oldest known light bulb was invented by Thomas Edison in 1879. The oldest known telephone was invented by Alexander Graham Bell in 1876. The oldest known radio transmission was by Guglielmo Marconi in 1895. The oldest known airplane flight by the Wright Brothers was in 1903. The oldest known automobile is Karl Benz's Motorwagen, patented in 1886. The oldest known computer, the Z3, was built by Konrad Zuse in 1941, but the first electronic general purpose computer was ENIAC in 1945. The oldest known programming language was Plankalkül, designed by Zuse in the 1940s, but Fortran came in 1957. The oldest known satellite, Sputnik 1, was launched by the Soviet Union in 1957. The oldest known human spaceflight, Yuri Gagarin, orbited Earth in 1961. The oldest known moon landing, Apollo 11, landed in 1969. The oldest known space station, Salyut 1, was launched by the Soviet Union in 1971. The oldest known internet precursor, ARPANET, began in 1969. The World Wide Web was invented by Tim Berners Lee in 1989. The oldest known smartphone, the IBM Simon, was released in 1994. The oldest known social media site, Six Degrees, launched in 1997. The oldest known search engine, Archie, was created in 1990, then Yahoo in 1994, then Google in 1998. The oldest known video game, Tennis for Two, was made in 1958. Pong was released in 1972. The oldest known home video game console, the Magnavox Odyssey, was released in 1972. The oldest known handheld game console, the Game Boy, was released in 1989. The oldest known digital camera was built by Steven Sasson at Kodak in 1975. The oldest known mobile phone call was made by Martin Cooper of Motorola in 1973. The oldest known artificial heart was implanted in 1982, the Jarvik 7. The oldest known cloning of a mammal, Dolly the sheep, was in 1996. The oldest known human genome sequenced was from 45,000 year old remains in Siberia. The oldest known DNA sequenced from a hominin was from a 430,000 year old Neanderthal. The oldest known fossil of Homo sapiens is 233,000 years old. The oldest known civilization in the Americas is the Caral Supe civilization in Peru, dating to 3500 BCE. The oldest known civilization in India is the Indus Valley Civilization, dating to 3300 BCE. The oldest known civilization in China is the Erlitou culture, dating to 1900 BCE, though the Xia dynasty is legendary from 2070 BCE. The oldest known empire is the Akkadian Empire, founded by Sargon of Akkad around 2334 BCE. The oldest known dynasty still extant is the Japanese imperial dynasty, traditionally founded in 660 BCE, historically from about 539 BCE. The oldest known continuously operating business is Kongo Gumi, a Japanese construction company founded in 578 CE, which operated until 2006. The oldest known still operating company is the Stiftskeller St. Peter restaurant in Salzburg, Austria, founded in 803 CE. The oldest known constitution is the Constitution of Medina, written in 622 CE by the Prophet Muhammad. The oldest written constitution still in effect is the United States Constitution, ratified in 1788. The oldest known treaty still in force is the Anglo Portuguese Alliance of 1373. The oldest known flag is the Dannebrog of Denmark, dating to 1219. The oldest known national anthem is the Wilhelmus of the Netherlands, written between 1568 and 1572. The oldest known monarchy still existing is the Japanese monarchy. The oldest known parliament is the Icelandic Althing, founded in 930 CE. The oldest known democracy is ancient Athens, around 508 BCE. The oldest known republic is the Republic of San Marino, founded in 301 CE. The oldest known city in the Americas is Caral, Peru, founded about 2600 BCE. The oldest known city in Africa is Thebes in Egypt, founded about 3200 BCE, but earlier settlements like Abydos date to 4000 BCE. The oldest known city in Europe is Plovdiv, Bulgaria, inhabited since about 6000 BCE. The oldest known city in Asia is Jericho, though it is in Asia, also Damascus is ancient. The oldest known city in Australia is Sydney, founded in 1788, but Aboriginal settlements are much older, though not cities in the urban sense. The oldest known university in Europe is the University of Bologna, founded in 1088. The oldest known medical school is the Schola Medica Salernitana in Italy, founded in the 9th century. The oldest known hospital is the Mihintale Hospital in Sri Lanka, dating to the 4th century BCE. The oldest known lighthouse is the Lighthouse of Alexandria, built around 280 BCE, one of the Seven Wonders. The oldest known still operating lighthouse is the Tower of Hercules in Spain, built in the 1st century CE. The oldest known bridge still in use is the Arkadiko Bridge in Greece, from 1300 BCE. The oldest known tunnel is the Tunnel of Eupalinos in Greece, built around 550 BCE. The oldest known dam is the Jawa Dam in Jordan, built around 3000 BCE. The oldest known aqueduct is the Aqueduct of Segovia in Spain, built around 50 CE, still in use. The oldest known road is the Lake Moeris Quarry Road in Egypt, built around 2600 BCE. The oldest known railway is the Diolkos across the Isthmus of Corinth, built around 600 BCE. The oldest known subway, the London Underground, opened in 1863. The oldest known skyscraper is the Home Insurance Building in Chicago, built in 1885, ten stories. The oldest known surviving building above ground is the Megalithic Temples of Malta, built around 3600 BCE. The oldest known stone circle is Nabta Playa in Egypt, built around 5000 BCE, older than Stonehenge. Stonehenge was built from 3000 to 2000 BCE. The oldest known pyramid is the Pyramid of Djoser in Egypt, built around 2630 BCE. The Great Pyramid of Giza was built around 2560 BCE. The oldest known palace is the Palace of Knossos in Crete, built around 1900 BCE. The oldest known theater is the Theater of Dionysus in Athens, built around 500 BCE. The oldest known stadium is the Stadium at Olympia in Greece, built around 450 BCE. The oldest known library still in operation is the Library of St. Catherine's Monastery in Egypt, founded around 565 CE. The oldest known museum is the Ennigaldi Nanna's museum in Ur, dating to 530 BCE. The oldest known zoo was in Hierakonpolis, Egypt, around 3500 BCE. The oldest known botanical garden was at the University of Pisa, founded in 1544. The oldest known clock tower is the Clock Tower of the Andronikos Kyrrhestes in Athens, built around 50 BCE. The oldest known sundial still working is the Georgian sundial at the Basilica of San Petronio in Bologna, built in 1655. The oldest known surviving newspaper is the Gazzetta di Mantova, founded in 1664. The oldest known surviving magazine is the Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society, first published in 1665. The oldest known patent was issued in Florence, Italy, in 1421. The oldest known trademark is the Bass red triangle, registered in the UK in 1876. The oldest known postage stamp is the Penny Black, issued in 1840. The oldest known currency still in use is the British pound sterling, dating back to Anglo Saxon times, 775 CE. The oldest known banking institution is the Banca Monte dei Paschi di Siena, founded in 1472, still operating. The oldest known stock exchange is the Amsterdam Stock Exchange, founded in 1602. The oldest known insurance company is Lloyd's of London, founded in 1686. The oldest known flag in continuous use is the Japanese flag, with design dating to the 8th century, though formal adoption was later. The oldest known anthem in continuous use is the Dutch Wilhelmus. The oldest known ruler whose name we know is Iry Hor, a pharaoh of Egypt around 3100 BCE, or perhaps King Scorpion I. The oldest known female ruler is Enheduanna, a Sumerian priestess and poet who lived around 2300 BCE, daughter of Sargon of Akkad. The oldest known war is the conflict between Sumerian city states, such as Lagash and Umma, around 2500 BCE. The oldest known peace treaty is the Egyptian Hittite treaty of 1259 BCE. The oldest known genocide is the destruction of Melos by Athens in 416 BCE, described by Thucydides. The oldest known pandemic is the Antonine Plague of 165 to 180 CE, likely smallpox. The oldest known vaccine was developed by Edward Jenner for smallpox in 1796. The oldest known antibiotic is penicillin, discovered by Alexander Fleming in 1928. The oldest known synthetic drug is chloral hydrate, synthesized in 1832. The oldest known anesthetic is diethyl ether, first used in surgery in 1846. The oldest known blood transfusion was in 1667, but successful modern transfusion was in 1901 after blood typing. The oldest known organ transplant was a kidney transplant in 1954. The oldest known artificial pacemaker was implanted in 1958. The oldest known MRI scan on a human was performed in 1977. The oldest known CT scan was in 1971. The oldest known in vitro fertilization baby, Louise Brown, was born in 1978. The oldest known human to have lived with verified age is Jeanne Calment, who died in 1997 at 122 years and 164 days. The oldest known living tree is a bristlecone pine named Methuselah, in California, over 4,850 years old. The oldest known living organism is a seagrass colony in the Mediterranean, Posidonia oceanica, estimated at 100,000 years old. The oldest known animal species still living is the horseshoe crab, unchanged for over 450 million years. The oldest known insect is the silverfish, unchanged for 400 million years. The oldest known bird species is the ostrich, which has existed for about 20 million years. The oldest known domesticated dog breed is the Saluki, dating to at least 7000 BCE. The oldest known cat breed is the Egyptian Mau, depicted in ancient Egyptian art. The oldest known horse breed is the Arabian horse, dating to 3000 BCE. The oldest known cow breed is the Ankole Watusi from Africa. The oldest known human occupation is the oldest profession, usually cited as prostitution, recorded in Sumerian texts. The oldest known tool still in widespread use is the needle, invented at least 25,000 years ago. The oldest known musical instrument still played in some form is the flute, though ancient bone flutes are not used. The oldest known board game is Senet, played in Egypt around 3100 BCE. The oldest known ball game is the Mesoamerican ballgame, dating to 1400 BCE. The oldest known Olympic Games were held in Olympia, Greece, in 776 BCE. The modern Olympics began in 1896. The oldest known marathon race was in 1896, following the legend of Pheidippides. The oldest known World Cup, the FIFA World Cup, began in 1930. The oldest known space object launched by humans still in orbit is Vanguard 1, launched in 1958. The oldest known human made object on the Moon is a Soviet flag from Luna 2 in 1959, though later missions left many objects. The oldest known message in a bottle was released in 1906 and found in 2006. The oldest known time capsule is the Detroit Century Box, sealed in 1900, but older examples exist like the Crypt of Civilization sealed in 1940. The oldest known living person in recorded history, not verified, is from ancient texts like Methuselah, but verified as Jeanne Calment. The oldest known verified living person as of 2025 is someone, but records change. The latest events in world general knowledge include the COVID 19 pandemic, declared in 2020, which infected hundreds of millions. The latest space missions include the James Webb Space Telescope launched in 2021, the Artemis program to return to the Moon, and Mars rovers Perseverance and Ingenuity. The latest Nobel Prizes are awarded annually. The latest scientific discoveries include gravitational waves detected in 2015, the first image of a black hole in 2019, and the discovery of phosphine in Venus's atmosphere in 2020, later contested. The latest world population milestone reached 8 billion in November 2022. The latest AI breakthroughs include large language models like GPT 4, image generators like DALL E 3, and AlphaFold for protein folding. The latest climate records include the hottest global average temperature in 2024. The latest political events include the Russian invasion of Ukraine in 2022, the Israel Hamas war in 2023, and various elections worldwide. The latest technological developments include quantum computing advances, CRISPR gene editing therapies approved, and renewable energy milestones. The latest sports events include the FIFA World Cup 2022 in Qatar, the 2024 Summer Olympics in Paris. The latest space tourism missions include Blue Origin and SpaceX flights. The latest deep sea discoveries include new species in the Mariana Trench. The latest archaeological finds include a 9,000 year old shrine in Jordan, and the oldest known figurative art from Indonesia dated to 51,000 years ago.
