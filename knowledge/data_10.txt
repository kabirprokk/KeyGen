The history of sports begins in prehistory. The oldest known sport is wrestling, with cave paintings in France depicting wrestlers dating to 15,300 years ago. Running is equally ancient, as humans have run for survival and competition for hundreds of thousands of years. The oldest known organized sporting event is the ancient Olympic Games, first held in Olympia, Greece, in 776 BCE. These games were held every four years for over a thousand years, until 393 CE when the Roman Emperor Theodosius I abolished them. The ancient Olympics included stadium running of about 192 meters, the diaulos of two stadium lengths, the dolichos of longer distance, wrestling, boxing, pankration which was a combination of wrestling and boxing with almost no rules, horse racing, chariot racing, the pentathlon which included discus, javelin, long jump, running, and wrestling. Only freeborn Greek men could compete, and they did so nude. Winners received olive wreaths. The ancient Panhellenic Games included the Pythian Games at Delphi, the Nemean Games, and the Isthmian Games. Chariot racing was the most popular sport in ancient Rome, held at the Circus Maximus which could seat 250,000 spectators. Gladiatorial combat, while more spectacle than sport, involved trained fighters in brutal combat. Roman ball games included harpastum, a rough ball game similar to rugby. In Mesoamerica, the Mesoamerican ballgame was played as early as 1400 BCE. The game used a solid rubber ball, and players used their hips, elbows, and knees to keep the ball in play. The losing team's captain was sometimes sacrificed. In ancient Egypt, swimming, fishing, javelin throwing, and archery were depicted in tomb paintings from 2000 BCE. In ancient China, a game called cuju was played from the 3rd century BCE. Cuju involved kicking a leather ball through a hole in a silk cloth. It is recognized by FIFA as the earliest form of football. In Japan, kemari was a ceremonial ball game where players kept a ball in the air without using hands, dating to the 6th century CE. In medieval Europe, mob football emerged, where entire villages would compete to move a ball to a target, with almost no rules. These games were violent and often banned. King Edward II banned football in London in 1314. Archery was mandated for all English men by law in the 13th century. Jousting and tournaments were the sports of knights, combining martial training with spectacle. Real tennis, also called royal tennis or court tennis, originated in 11th century France and is the ancestor of modern tennis. It is played indoors on a complex asymmetrical court. The oldest surviving tennis court is at Hampton Court Palace, built in 1530. Golf originated in Scotland in the 15th century. The first written record of golf is from 1457 when King James II banned it because it distracted men from archery. The oldest golf course still in use is the Old Course at St Andrews, used since 1552. Bowling has ancient roots, with evidence of a similar game in ancient Egypt, but modern lawn bowling developed in 13th century England. Cricket originated in England in the 16th century. The first known match was played in 1646. The first international cricket match was between Canada and the United States in 1844. The Ashes series between England and Australia began in 1882. Modern boxing evolved from bare knuckle fighting. The first recorded bare knuckle fight in England was in 1681. The London Prize Ring rules were established in 1743. The Marquess of Queensberry Rules, with gloves and three minute rounds, were published in 1867. The first world heavyweight champion under these rules was John L Sullivan in 1885. Horse racing has ancient roots but modern thoroughbred racing began in 17th century England. The Epsom Derby began in 1780. The Kentucky Derby began in 1875. The Melbourne Cup began in 1861. The modern Olympic Games were revived in 1896 by Pierre de Coubertin. The first modern Olympics were held in Athens, Greece, with 241 athletes from 14 nations competing in 43 events. The 1900 Paris Olympics included women for the first time. The 1904 St Louis Olympics were poorly organized but introduced gold, silver, and bronze medals. The 1908 London Olympics saw the first opening ceremony. The 1912 Stockholm Olympics introduced electronic timing. The 1920 Antwerp Olympics featured the Olympic flag. The 1924 Paris Olympics saw the first Olympic Village. The 1936 Berlin Olympics were used for Nazi propaganda but featured Jesse Owens winning four gold medals. The 1948 London Olympics were the first after World War II. The 1960 Rome Olympics were the first televised. The 1968 Mexico City Olympics saw Tommie Smith and John Carlos give the Black Power salute. The 1972 Munich Olympics were marred by the terrorist attack on Israeli athletes. The 1980 Moscow Olympics were boycotted by the US and allies. The 1984 Los Angeles Olympics were boycotted by the Soviet bloc. The 1992 Barcelona Olympics allowed professional basketball players, creating the Dream Team. The 2000 Sydney Olympics were highly successful. The 2008 Beijing Olympics were the most expensive ever. The 2016 Rio Olympics were the first in South America. The 2020 Tokyo Olympics were postponed to 2021 due to the COVID 19 pandemic. The Winter Olympics began in 1924 in Chamonix, France. Events include alpine skiing, cross country skiing, ski jumping, biathlon which combines skiing and shooting, figure skating, speed skating, ice hockey, bobsleigh, luge, skeleton, snowboarding, and freestyle skiing. The Paralympic Games began in 1960 in Rome for athletes with disabilities. The Special Olympics began in 1968. Association football, or soccer, is the world's most popular sport. The Football Association was founded in England in 1863, establishing the first standardized rules. The FA Cup, the oldest football competition, began in 1871. The first international match was between England and Scotland in 1872. The English Football League began in 1888. FIFA was founded in 1904. The FIFA World Cup began in 1930 in Uruguay, won by Uruguay. The World Cup has been held every four years except 1942 and 1946 due to World War II. Brazil has won five times, the most. Germany and Italy have won four times each. Argentina has won three times. France and Uruguay have won twice. England and Spain have won once. The women's FIFA World Cup began in 1991, won by the United States, which has won the most with four titles. The UEFA Champions League began as the European Cup in 1955, rebranded in 1992. Real Madrid has won the most titles, fourteen as of 2025. The Copa America is the oldest international continental football competition, first held in 1916. The Africa Cup of Nations began in 1957. The Asian Cup began in 1956. The CONCACAF Gold Cup began in 1991. The Oceania Nations Cup began in 1973. American football evolved from rugby and soccer. The first college football game was between Princeton and Rutgers in 1869. The NFL was founded in 1920 as the American Professional Football Association. The Super Bowl began in 1967, merging the NFL and AFL champions. The Pittsburgh Steelers and New England Patriots have won the most Super Bowls, six each. The Super Bowl is the most watched television event in the United States annually. Baseball evolved from English rounders. The first recorded baseball game was in 1846 in Hoboken, New Jersey. The Knickerbocker Rules were written by Alexander Cartwright in 1845. The first professional team was the Cincinnati Red Stockings in 1869. The National League was founded in 1876. The American League was founded in 1901. The World Series began in 1903. The New York Yankees have won the most World Series titles, 27. Jackie Robinson broke the color barrier in 1947. The Negro Leagues operated from 1920 to 1960, featuring legendary players like Satchel Paige and Josh Gibson. Major League Baseball integrated fully by 1959. The designated hitter rule was adopted by the American League in 1973 and by the National League in 2022. The wild card was introduced in 1994. Instant replay was introduced in 2008. The World Baseball Classic began in 2006. Basketball was invented by Dr. James Naismith in Springfield, Massachusetts, in 1891. He hung a peach basket at each end of a gymnasium and wrote 13 rules. The first professional league was the National Basketball League in 1898. The NBA was founded in 1946 as the Basketball Association of America, merging with the NBL in 1949 to become the NBA. The Boston Celtics have won the most NBA championships, 17, tied with the Los Angeles Lakers also 17 as of 2025. Michael Jordan won six championships with the Chicago Bulls. LeBron James has won four championships with three different teams. The three point line was introduced by the NBA in 1979. The WNBA for women began in 1997. FIBA, the international basketball federation, was founded in 1932. Basketball became an Olympic sport in 1936. The Dream Team of 1992, featuring Michael Jordan, Magic Johnson, Larry Bird, and others, dominated the Barcelona Olympics. Ice hockey originated in Canada in the 19th century. The first organized indoor game was in Montreal in 1875. The Stanley Cup was first awarded in 1893, making it the oldest professional sports trophy in North America. The NHL was founded in 1917. The Montreal Canadiens have won the most Stanley Cups, 24. Wayne Gretzky, called The Great One, holds the most career points with 2,857. The Soviet national team dominated international hockey from the 1950s to 1990s. The Miracle on Ice occurred at the 1980 Winter Olympics when the US college team beat the Soviet team. Rugby football originated at Rugby School in England in 1823 when William Webb Ellis picked up the ball and ran. The Rugby Football Union was founded in 1871. Rugby league split from rugby union in 1895 over payment issues. The Rugby World Cup for union began in 1987, won by New Zealand. South Africa has won the most titles, four as of 2025. The Rugby World Cup for league began in 1954. The Six Nations Championship began as the Home Nations in 1883, becoming the Five Nations in 1910 when France joined, and the Six Nations when Italy joined in 2000. The Rugby Championship, formerly the Tri Nations, began in 1996. Tennis evolved from real tennis. Modern lawn tennis was invented by Major Walter Wingfield in 1873. The first Wimbledon Championship was held in 1877, the oldest tennis tournament. The US Open began in 1881. The French Open began in 1891. The Australian Open began in 1905. These four are the Grand Slam tournaments. Rod Laver won two calendar year Grand Slams in 1962 and 1969. Margaret Court holds the record for most Grand Slam singles titles with 24. Novak Djokovic holds the men's record with 24 as of 2025. Serena Williams holds the Open Era record for women with 23. The Davis Cup, the men's international team competition, began in 1900. The Fed Cup for women began in 1963. The Open Era began in 1968, allowing professionals to compete in Grand Slams. Golf's modern rules were formalized by the Royal and Ancient Golf Club of St Andrews in 1754. The Open Championship, called the British Open, first played in 1860, is the oldest major championship. The US Open began in 1895. The PGA Championship began in 1916. The Masters began in 1934. These four are the men's major championships. Jack Nicklaus has won the most majors with 18. Tiger Woods has won 15. The women's majors include the Chevron Championship, the US Women's Open, the Women's PGA Championship, and the Evian Championship. Annika Sorenstam has won 10 majors. The Ryder Cup, between the United States and Europe, began in 1927. The Presidents Cup between the United States and an International team began in 1994. Swimming has been an Olympic sport since 1896 for men and 1912 for women. Michael Phelps has won the most Olympic medals of any athlete, 28 total, including 23 golds. Katie Ledecky holds multiple world records in distance freestyle. The FINA World Aquatics Championships began in 1973. Diving, water polo, synchronized swimming, and open water swimming are also Olympic disciplines. Athletics, or track and field, has been in the Olympics since 1896. Usain Bolt holds the world records for 100 meters at 9.58 seconds, 200 meters at 19.19 seconds. Carl Lewis won 9 Olympic gold medals in sprints and long jump. Allyson Felix is the most decorated American track athlete with 11 Olympic medals. The World Athletics Championships began in 1983. The Boston Marathon began in 1897, the oldest annual marathon. The New York City Marathon began in 1970. The Abbott World Marathon Majors include Boston, New York, Chicago, London, Berlin, and Tokyo. Eliud Kipchoge is the marathon world record holder at 2 hours 35 seconds. He ran under 2 hours in a non record breaking event in 2019. Cycling began as a sport in the 19th century. The Tour de France began in 1903, the most famous bicycle race. Eddy Merckx is considered the greatest cyclist, with 5 Tour de France wins, 11 Grand Tours total. The Giro d'Italia began in 1909. The Vuelta a España began in 1935. These are the three Grand Tours. Track cycling has been an Olympic sport since 1896. Mountain biking became an Olympic sport in 1996. BMX racing became Olympic in 2008. Boxing has been an Olympic sport since 1904, though ancient boxing was in the ancient Olympics. Professional boxing has multiple governing bodies, including the WBA founded in 1921, WBC in 1963, IBF in 1983, and WBO in 1988. Muhammad Ali, born Cassius Clay, won the world heavyweight championship three times and is considered the greatest. Mike Tyson became the youngest heavyweight champion at 20 years old. Floyd Mayweather retired undefeated at 50 and 0. Manny Pacquiao won world championships in eight different weight classes. Mixed martial arts, or MMA, emerged in the early 1990s. The Ultimate Fighting Championship, or UFC, was founded in 1993. The first UFC event had few rules and pitted different martial arts styles against each other. Royce Gracie won early tournaments with Brazilian Jiu Jitsu. The unified rules of MMA were adopted in the 2000s. Weight classes were established. Conor McGregor became the first fighter to hold two UFC championships simultaneously. Jon Jones is widely considered the greatest MMA fighter of all time. The UFC became a mainstream sport under the leadership of Dana White. Volleyball was invented by William G. Morgan in 1895 as a less intense alternative to basketball. Beach volleyball became an Olympic sport in 1996. Indoor volleyball has been Olympic since 1964. The FIVB Volleyball World Championships began in 1949 for men and 1952 for women. Handball is a fast paced team sport. Field handball was introduced at the 1936 Olympics. Indoor handball became Olympic in 1972 for men and 1976 for women. The IHF World Men's Handball Championship began in 1938. Water polo is one of the oldest team sports in the Olympics, introduced in 1900. It is played in a pool, with teams trying to throw a ball into the opponent's goal. Field hockey has been Olympic since 1908 for men and 1980 for women. The Hockey World Cup began in 1971 for men and 1974 for women. India has dominated men's field hockey, winning 8 Olympic gold medals. Cricket is the second most popular sport in the world after football. Test cricket, the longest format, began with the first Test match between Australia and England in 1877. One Day International cricket, or ODI, began in 1971. The Cricket World Cup, an ODI tournament, began in 1975. Australia has won the most World Cups, 5 as of 2025. Twenty20 cricket, or T20, began in 2003. The T20 World Cup began in 2007. The Indian Premier League, or IPL, began in 2008 and is the most lucrative T20 league. Don Bradman of Australia is considered the greatest batsman of all time, with a Test batting average of 99.94. Sachin Tendulkar of India holds the record for most international runs and centuries. Rugby sevens is a faster variant of rugby union with seven players per side instead of fifteen. It became an Olympic sport in 2016. The World Rugby Sevens Series began in 1999. Bowling, specifically ten pin bowling, is played by millions worldwide. The Professional Bowlers Association, or PBA, was founded in 1958. The USBC Masters and PBA World Championship are major tournaments. Walter Ray Williams Jr. holds the record for most PBA titles with 47. Snooker is a cue sport derived from billiards. The World Snooker Championship began in 1927, held at the Crucible Theatre in Sheffield since 1977. Stephen Hendry and Ronnie O'Sullivan have each won 7 world titles. O'Sullivan is considered the most talented player ever. Darts has professional tournaments organized by the Professional Darts Corporation and the British Darts Organisation. The PDC World Darts Championship began in 1994. Phil Taylor is the most successful player, with 16 world titles. Billiards, or pool, has many variants including eight ball, nine ball, and straight pool. The World Pool Championship began in 1990. Baseball and softball returned to the Olympics after being dropped. Baseball was Olympic from 1992 to 2008 and returned in 2020. Softball was Olympic from 1996 to 2008 and returned in 2020. Karate and taekwondo are martial arts turned Olympic sports. Taekwondo became Olympic in 2000. Karate was added for 2020 but dropped for 2024. Judo became Olympic in 1964 for men and 1992 for women. The IJF World Judo Championships began in 1956. Wrestling, both freestyle and Greco Roman, has been Olympic since 1896. Greco Roman wrestling prohibits holds below the waist. Freestyle wrestling allows leg holds. The UWW World Wrestling Championships began in 1904 for freestyle and 1905 for Greco Roman. Weightlifting has been Olympic since 1896. Athletes compete in snatch and clean and jerk. The IWF World Championships began in 1891. Fencing has been Olympic since 1896. The three weapons are foil, epee, and sabre. The FIE World Fencing Championships began in 1921. Archery has ancient origins and returned to the Olympics in 1972 after being dropped. The World Archery Championships began in 1931. Shooting has been Olympic since 1896, except 1904 and 1928. The ISSF World Shooting Championships began in 1897. Triathlon combines swimming, cycling, and running. It became Olympic in 2000. The Ironman World Championship in Hawaii began in 1978, covering a 2.4 mile swim, 112 mile bike, and 26.2 mile run. The ITU World Triathlon Series began in 2009. Modern pentathlon was created by Pierre de Coubertin for the Olympics, first held in 1912. It includes fencing, swimming, equestrian show jumping, pistol shooting, and cross country running. Skateboarding, sport climbing, and surfing debuted at the 2020 Tokyo Olympics. BMX freestyle also debuted. Breaking, a form of street dance, debuted at the 2024 Paris Olympics. The X Games began in 1995, showcasing extreme sports like skateboarding, BMX, motocross, and snowboarding. The Winter X Games began in 1997. Snowboarding became Olympic in 1998. Shaun White won three Olympic gold medals in halfpipe. Freestyle skiing became Olympic in 1992. Moguls were added in 1992, aerials in 1994, ski cross in 2010, and slopestyle and halfpipe in 2014. Speed skating has been Olympic since 1924. Short track speed skating was added as a demonstration sport in 1988 and official in 1992. Eric Heiden won five gold medals in speed skating at the 1980 Olympics. Figure skating has been Olympic since 1908 for pairs and 1924 for singles. Ice dancing was added in 1976. Sonja Henie won three Olympic gold medals. Michelle Kwan won two Olympic medals but no gold. Yuzuru Hanyu won two men's gold medals. Bobsleigh has been Olympic since 1924 for four man and 1932 for two man. Women's bobsleigh was added in 2002. Luge has been Olympic since 1964. Skeleton was Olympic in 1928 and 1948, then returned in 2002. Alpine skiing has been Olympic since 1936. Downhill, slalom, giant slalom, super G, and combined are the disciplines. Jean Claude Killy won three golds in 1968. Mikaela Shiffrin has the most World Cup wins of any skier. Cross country skiing has been Olympic since 1924. Marit Bjørgen holds the record for most Winter Olympic medals with 15. Ski jumping has been Olympic since 1924. The ski flying world championships began in 1972. Nordic combined combines cross country skiing and ski jumping, Olympic since 1924. Biathlon combines cross country skiing and rifle shooting, Olympic since 1960 for men and 1992 for women. Ole Einar Bjørndalen has 13 Olympic medals in biathlon. Motorsports are a major category of sport. Formula One began in 1950, the highest class of single seater auto racing. The FIA World Drivers Championship has been won by Lewis Hamilton with 7 titles, tied with Michael Schumacher. The Monaco Grand Prix, first held in 1929, is the most famous race. NASCAR, the National Association for Stock Car Auto Racing, began in 1948. The Daytona 500 began in 1959. Richard Petty and Dale Earnhardt each have 7 NASCAR Cup championships. Jimmie Johnson also has 7. IndyCar racing evolved from open wheel racing in the United States. The Indianapolis 500 began in 1911, the oldest major auto race. MotoGP, the top class of motorcycle road racing, began as the Motorcycle World Championship in 1949. Giacomo Agostini has 15 world championships. Valentino Rossi has 9. Rally racing is held on closed public roads. The World Rally Championship began in 1973. Sébastien Loeb has 9 world championships. Drag racing involves two cars racing in a straight line. The NHRA was founded in 1951. Equestrian sports have been Olympic since 1900. Dressage, show jumping, and eventing are the disciplines. The FEI World Equestrian Games began in 1990. The Kentucky Derby, first run in 1875, is the most famous horse race in the United States. It is the first leg of the Triple Crown, followed by the Preakness Stakes and Belmont Stakes. The Grand National, first run in 1839, is the most famous steeplechase. Sailing has been Olympic since 1900, except 1904. The America's Cup began in 1851, the oldest international sporting trophy. The Volvo Ocean Race, now The Ocean Race, began in 1973. Rowing has been Olympic since 1900 for men and 1976 for women. The Oxford and Cambridge Boat Race began in 1829. The Henley Royal Regatta began in 1839. The Head of the Charles Regatta began in 1965. Canoeing and kayaking have been Olympic since 1936. Slalom events were added in 1972. The ICF Canoe Sprint World Championships began in 1938. Weight training as a sport includes powerlifting and Olympic weightlifting. Powerlifting consists of squat, bench press, and deadlift. The IPF World Championships began in 1971. Strongman competitions, such as the World's Strongest Man, began in 1977. Magnus Ver Magnusson, Mariusz Pudzianowski, and Hafthor Bjornsson are multiple winners. Bodybuilding competitions like Mr. Olympia began in 1965. Arnold Schwarzenegger won 7 Mr. Olympia titles. Ronny Coleman won 8. Lee Haney won 8. CrossFit began in 2000. The CrossFit Games began in 2007. The fittest athletes compete in varied functional movements. Chess is recognized as a sport by the International Olympic Committee. The World Chess Championship began officially in 1886. Garry Kasparov held the title from 1985 to 2000. Magnus Carlsen, the highest rated player in history, held the title from 2013 to 2023. Chess Olympiads began in 1924. Esports, competitive video gaming, emerged in the late 1990s and exploded in the 2010s. Games include League of Legends, Dota 2, Counter Strike, Valorant, Overwatch, Fortnite, and many others. The International, Dota 2's premier tournament, began in 2011 with the largest prize pools in esports history. The League of Legends World Championship began in 2011. The Olympics has considered adding esports. The Asian Games included esports as a medal event in 2022. Cue sports including snooker, pool, and billiards have world championships. The World Pool Billiard Association governs many events. The Mosconi Cup, a team pool competition between the US and Europe, began in 1994. Lawn bowls is a precision sport. The World Bowls Championships began in 1966. It has been in the Commonwealth Games since 1930. Squash evolved from racquets. The PSA World Championship began in 1976. Squash has been added to the Olympics for 2028. Racquetball is a similar sport played indoors. The IRF World Championships began in 1981. Badminton has been Olympic since 1992. It originated from the British India game of battledore and shuttlecock. The BWF World Championships began in 1977. Lin Dan is considered the greatest male badminton player. Table tennis has been Olympic since 1988. It originated in Victorian England as indoor tennis. The ITTF World Championships began in 1926. Ma Long is considered the greatest male table tennis player. Futsal is a variant of association football played indoors on a smaller field. The FIFA Futsal World Cup began in 1989. Beach soccer is played on sand. The FIFA Beach Soccer World Cup began in 2005. Sepak Takraw, also called kick volleyball, originated in Southeast Asia. Players use their feet, knees, chest, and head to get a ball over a net. The King's Cup in Thailand is the premier event. Kabaddi originated in ancient India. The Pro Kabaddi League began in 2014. The Kabaddi World Cup began in 2004. Kho kho is another traditional Indian tag sport. Ultimate Frisbee, now called Ultimate, is a non contact team sport using a flying disc. The World Flying Disc Federation World Championships began in 1983. Ultimate became an Olympic sport for 2028. Dodgeball is played recreationally and in organized leagues. The World Dodgeball Federation was founded in 2008. Roller sports include roller derby, inline speed skating, and artistic roller skating. Roller derby has the Women's Flat Track Derby Association. Speed skating on roller skates has World Championships since 1937. Lumberjack sports, also called wood chopping, include axe throwing, log rolling, and chainsaw carving. The Stihl Timbersports Series began in 1985. Highland games are traditional Scottish events including caber toss, hammer throw, and tug of war. The Braemar Gathering has been held since 1832. Highland games occur worldwide. Strongest man competitions include the Arnold Strongman Classic and World's Strongest Man. Hafthor Bjornsson won the World's Strongest Man in 2018 and set a deadlift world record of 501 kilograms in 2020. Sports records are tracked meticulously. The oldest known sports record is the Olympic record of Leonidas of Rhodes, who won 12 running titles in 4 consecutive Olympics from 164 to 152 BCE. The longest winning streak in professional sports is the UCLA men's basketball team's 88 game win streak from 1971 to 1974. The most consecutive wins in tennis is 46 by Novak Djokovic in 2010 to 2011. The longest individual winning streak in boxing is Joe Louis with 25 successful heavyweight title defenses from 1937 to 1949. The most goals scored in a single football match is 31, an Australian record. The highest score in a Test cricket innings is 400 not out by Brian Lara. The most points scored in a single NBA game by one player is 100 by Wilt Chamberlain in 1962. The most home runs in a single MLB season is 73 by Barry Bonds in 2001, though his career is tainted by doping allegations. The most Olympic gold medals won by one person is 23 by Michael Phelps. The most Paralympic gold medals is 22 by Trischa Zorn. The most world championships in a single sport is by Michael Schumacher and Lewis Hamilton with 7 Formula One titles. The oldest athlete to win an Olympic gold medal is Oscar Swahn, who won a shooting gold at 64 years old in 1912. The youngest Olympic gold medalist is Marjorie Gestring, a 13 year old diver in 1936. The oldest professional athlete to play in a major league is Satchel Paige, who pitched in MLB at 59 years old. The highest paid sport is Formula One or basketball depending on metrics. The most attended sporting event is the Kumbh Mela, a religious festival with sporting elements, but for a single stadium, the 1950 FIFA World Cup final had an estimated 199,000 spectators. The Super Bowl consistently has over 100 million television viewers in the United States. The FIFA World Cup final is watched by over 1.5 billion people globally. Sports technology has evolved enormously. Video replay, goal line technology, Hawk Eye, and VAR have changed officiating. Wearable technology tracks athlete performance. Doping is a persistent problem. The World Anti Doping Agency, WADA, was founded in 1999. The Tour de France has been plagued by doping scandals, notably Lance Armstrong being stripped of his 7 titles. Baseball's steroid era included Mark McGwire, Sammy Sosa, and Barry Bonds. The BALCO scandal implicated many athletes. Russian state sponsored doping led to sanctions and bans from the Olympics. The 2034 FIFA World Cup will be in Saudi Arabia. New sports continue to emerge. Pickleball, a paddle sport combining tennis, badminton, and ping pong, is the fastest growing sport in the United States. Padel is a racquet sport growing rapidly in Europe and Latin America. Footgolf combines soccer and golf. Disc golf uses flying discs thrown into baskets. Bossaball combines volleyball, soccer, and gymnastics on inflatables. Drone racing, competitive drone flying through obstacle courses, has professional leagues. Competitive eating, while controversial, has the Nathan's Hot Dog Eating Contest and Major League Eating.
